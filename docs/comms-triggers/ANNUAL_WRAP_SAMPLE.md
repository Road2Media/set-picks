## When those facts are missing

If no played bustout has a gap, the shared race fact is how often the annual lead changed hands. If that count cannot be computed and the calendar has a last venue, the race fact is the closing show. If none of those exist, the race sentence stays “Calling setlists is an inexact science…” and the crowd paragraph still appears when its own facts exist.

A song named on fewer than 10 cards is not given a hit rate. One qualifying song is not called both the highest and the lowest. A night with no official setlist is left out of the crowd count.
